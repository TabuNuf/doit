# T-028.11: doit epic add writes the new epic header: In short and Why it exists above Status

## Description

### Goal
A new epic starts in the shape the standard asks for, so nobody rewrites its header by hand.

### Decided
1. `epicTemplate` in src/store/markdown.js writes under the title: `In short: <the goal in one sentence>`, `Why it exists: <what was seen, when, and what replaces it>`, a blank line, `Status: <status>`, then `Design: Design.md, section <name>`.
2. An epic without a game gets no `Goal:` line. A campaign epic (it has a game) gets neither new line and keeps Game, Event and its brief lines under Status, as today.
3. Sections, in order: Findings, Tasks, Test plan, Notes, Close audit, For the standard.
