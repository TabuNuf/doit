# T-026.7: Check in play

## Description

As the designer,
I want to see a parked or closed epic take its open tasks off my lists,
so that I know the overview is clean before the epic closes.

### What to do
1. Drag a Planned epic that has open tasks onto Parked. Its tasks leave Active and Backlog, and the Claude view and the project page stop listing them (T-026.1, .4, .5).
2. Click the parked epic. Its tasks are listed, under a line that says why they show only here and how to bring them back (T-026.6).
3. Drag it back onto the open epics. The tasks are back, each in the state it had (T-026.1).
4. In a project you can spare, close an epic that still has an open task, then drag it back onto the open epics. The task was not archived and is back as it was (T-026.2).
5. In a terminal, `node bin/doit.js list` leaves a parked epic's tasks out, `list --epic <id>` lists them and `show` says the task is hidden (T-026.3).

Source: EPIC-026, Findings.
