# T-033.11: The weekly list picks its contacts by rating

## Description

Goal: This week names the five people most worth writing to, in place of the oldest due date.

In `src/domain/outreachPlan.js`, `planWeek` takes `rated` (per game, the `Ratings.forGame` rows) and `beats` (per game, the next beat's date or none). Contacts: open campaign tasks with a contact stay first. Then rated people by tier, then those who replied before, then points, then reach, cut to 5 in all. Leave out anyone with a touch in the last 56 days and anyone on an open campaign task. A game more than 42 days from its next beat, or with no beat, offers only `strong`; within 42 days also `good`; never `long shot`. Each row carries `tier`, `parts` and `persona`. The due-date path is removed with its tests. `Outreach.week()` gets both inputs through a ratings port the root passes; `outreachLines` prints the tier.

56 and 42 are named constants; where T-033.4 changed them or kept Next contact, follow the epic's Specification. Do not change the festivals and steps parts.

Run `npm test` and report its output. The task is done only when it passes.
