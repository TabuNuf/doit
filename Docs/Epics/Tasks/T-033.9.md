# T-033.9: Group people as shown, hidden or not researched

## Description

Goal: people who cover no game of our scale leave the lists without being parked by hand, and unresearched people stop looking judged.

Add `src/domain/contactGroups.js`, pure: `groupOf(contact, profiles)` returns `{group, reason}`. `unresearched`: no scales and no covered games. `hidden`: scales or covered games exist and none shares a scale label with any profile (reason `covers only <labels>`), or the contact has channels and every one is dead (reason `every channel is dead`). Otherwise `shown`. A contact with a fit or `special` is always `shown`. An event has no group. `ContactBook.list` adds `group` and `groupReason` to each person and, as with parked, leaves hidden people out unless the filter has `hidden: true` (only them) or `all`; `unresearched: true` keeps only those. Add `--hidden` and `--unresearched` to `doit contacts`, `hidden=1` and `unresearched=1` to `GET /api/contacts`, and `help.js`.

`contactBook.js` is 278 lines: the logic stays in the new file. Do not change `status` or `relevance`.

Run `npm test` and report its output. The task is done only when it passes.
